Program overview

A course that treats spatial computing like a product craft

Beyond Reality is designed for ambitious builders who want to understand how machines interpret 3D space and how AI makes those experiences smarter. The curriculum moves from WebAR fundamentals into computer vision, voice, generative tools, spatial UI, and industry applications.

Instead of staying at theory level, every module asks learners to ship something: scenes, prototypes, integration demos, architecture notes, and finally a capstone that can be shared publicly.

  • Hello-world to production-ready progression
  • Direct exposure to AI APIs, WebAR tooling, and spatial design workflows
  • Intentional room for future custom course visuals and live project showcases
